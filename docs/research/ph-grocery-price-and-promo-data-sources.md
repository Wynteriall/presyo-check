# Philippine Grocery Price & Promo Data Sources — Feasibility Audit

- **Ticket:** [Wynteriall/presyo-check#3](https://github.com/Wynteriall/presyo-check/issues/3) (Parent Map: [#1](https://github.com/Wynteriall/presyo-check/issues/1), Unblocks: [#6](https://github.com/Wynteriall/presyo-check/issues/6))
- **Audit Date:** November 24, 2026
- **Methodology:** Direct primary-source verification — live non-invasive HTTP inspection (`HEAD`/`GET`, `robots.txt`, XML/TXT sitemaps, rendered HTML metadata, first-party Terms of Use), download and text parsing of the latest DTI S3 SRP PDF bulletin, live Open Food Facts API probes on Philippine (`480...`) barcodes, and official OCR/Gemini API documentation review. No CAPTCHAs, Cloudflare/WAF challenges, or authentication gates were bypassed; every encountered block is recorded as a primary feasibility finding.

---

## Executive Summary

1. **No major Philippine grocery retailer offers a public, ToS-compliant pricing API, and automated scraping is non-viable for a production-path $0 app.** Every major chain with a live online catalog either explicitly prohibits automated access/scraping in its `robots.txt` or Terms of Use (`smmarkets.ph`, `waltermartdelivery.com.ph`, `snrshopping.com`, `landers.ph`), hydrates prices strictly behind client-side SPAs and branch/membership sessions, or sits behind Cloudflare/WAF protections.
2. **Half of the target Philippine retail roster has no first-party web catalog for walk-in grocery prices at all.**
   - **Robinsons Supermarket's** proprietary online storefronts (`gorobinsons.ph` and `gocart.ph`) are **discontinued/dead** (TLS errors); only marketing promo flyers remain on `robinsonssupermarket.com.ph`.
   - **Puregold** (`puregold.com.ph`) is a corporate/investor-relations site; online grocery shopping ("Sally") is locked inside its native mobile app.
   - **Alfamart** (`alfamart.com.ph`) has only 6 static corporate pages; promos exist only on Facebook/Viber posters and partial third-party listings.
   - **Dali** (`dali.ph`) has zero retail catalog; its Shopify bulk portal (`dalidash.ph`) covers only **96 wholesale case/carton SKUs**, not its full single-piece in-store assortment.
   - **7-Eleven's** `cliqqshop.com` exposes structured SSR prices, but it is a **ship-to-store e-commerce warehouse catalog** (Luzon/GMM pickup with heavy online markdowns), **not** walk-in convenience store shelf pricing.
3. **Delivery aggregators (GrabMart, Foodpanda, MetroMart, Pickaroo) are a dead end for primary sourcing.** GrabMart (`HTTP 403` CloudFront) and Foodpanda (`HTTP 403` Cloudflare Bot Management) hard-block automated requests immediately, all four prohibit scraping in ToS, and merchant-controlled app menus routinely carry **15%–33% commission markups** that contradict real walk-in shelf prices.
4. **DTI SRP and Open Food Facts are useful supporting layers, not primary price feeds.** DTI's interactive `epresyo.dti.gov.ph` portal is currently unreachable/timed out; DTI instead publishes static S3-hosted A3 poster PDFs (latest verified: **May 11, 2026**, **167 price entries** across statutory basic necessities/prime commodities, updated ~1–2×/year). Open Food Facts covers ~9,490 Philippine products under ODbL, but enforces tight rate limits (15 req/min product reads, 10 req/min search), sheds anonymous search traffic with `HTTP 503`, and exhibits severe crowdsourced unit-size errors (e.g., Lucky Me Beef 55g listed as `quantity: "5l"`).
5. **Recommended $0-sustainable v1 architecture (input for #6):** Build a **Hybrid Evidence-Backed Pipeline** — (a) **Curated Canonical Seed** (150–300 core PH pantry SKUs + private-label substitutes anchored by DTI SRP & cleaned Open Food Facts bulk dumps), (b) **Crowdsourced Shelf/Receipt Updates** using **on-device OCR** (Google ML Kit v2 on native React or Tesseract.js in web/PWA) paired with user verification + staleness badges, and (c) **Curated Weekly Promo Flyer Ingestion** using **Gemini Developer API Free Tier (`gemini-3.5-flash-lite`) Structured Outputs** to turn public store promo posters (SM, Puregold, Robinsons, Dali, Alfamart, Landers, S&R) into normalized deal records with 1-click review.

## Detailed Source-by-Source Audit

### 1. Supermarkets

#### Savemore / SM Markets Online (`smmarkets.ph`)
- **Platform & Catalog Availability:** `shop.smmarkets.ph` permanently redirects (`301`) to `https://smmarkets.ph/`, a unified **Magento 2 PWA Studio (`venia-static`)** storefront behind Cloudflare covering **SM Supermarket, SM Hypermarket, and Savemore**. Its XML sitemap index (`https://smmarkets.ph/media/sitemap.xml`, updated Nov 22, 2026) references `sitemap-1-1.xml` (7,128 URLs) and `sitemap-1-2.xml` (7,994 URLs) — **15,122 catalog/category URLs total**. However, initial HTTP responses return a minimal client-side SPA shell; product data and branch-specific pricing hydrate dynamically via JavaScript/GraphQL after branch selection.
- **Deals & Promos Visibility:** Strong digital promo footprint visible directly in the sitemap — including chain-segmented promo pages such as `/promos.html`, `/promos/sm-price-drop-svi-smco.html` (Savemore), `/promos/sm-price-drop-ssm.html` (SM Supermarket), `/promos/payday-bonus.html`, `/promos/bundle-save.html`, and `/promos/11-11-deals.html`.
- **Price Accuracy vs. In-Store:** SM e-commerce Terms explicitly note that online prices may vary from walk-in branch prices, and online-exclusive vouchers or in-store clearance/SMAC (SM Advantage Card) discounts do not automatically overlap. Format-level variance also exists across SM's three banners: Savemore carries a smaller neighborhood assortment, SM Supermarket runs flagship mall promos, and SM Hypermarket frequently offers wholesale/multi-pack pricing tiers, alongside Metro Manila vs. provincial freight differences.
- **Legal/ToS & Technical Constraints:** `https://smmarkets.ph/robots.txt` **explicitly disallows** `/catalog/`, `/catalog/category/view/`, `/catalog/product/view/`, `/catalogsearch/`, `/*___store=*`, and `/*search.html?query*`. Combined with Cloudflare and standard SM anti-scraping ToS clauses, automated crawling is both non-compliant and brittle.
- **$0 Sustainability Verdict:** Do **not** build an automated scraper against `smmarkets.ph`. Include Savemore / SM Markets in PresyoCheck v1 via curated baseline seeding, public SM Markets promo poster extraction (SM Price Drop / Payday circulars), and crowdsourced in-store receipts.

#### Puregold (`puregold.com.ph` & Puregold Mobile / "Sally")
- **Platform & Catalog Availability:** `https://www.puregold.com.ph/` is a **Joomla** corporate and investor-relations portal (SEC filings, corporate governance, privacy policy) with **zero online product catalog or pricing**. Candidate subdomains (`shop.puregold.com.ph`, `sally.puregold.com.ph`) do not resolve (`DNS NXDOMAIN`). Puregold's e-commerce grocery catalog, barcode scanner, and pricing live exclusively inside the **Puregold Mobile App ("Sally" — Shopping Ally)** on Android and iOS, while wholesale sari-sari store ordering lives in the separate **Aling Puring App** (*Tindahan ni Aling Puring* / TNAP).
- **Deals & Promos Visibility:** Regular consumer deals appear as **"Sally's Deals"** and Voucher Pocket promos inside the mobile app, plus in-store Puregold Perks / Aling Puring circulars and official Facebook announcements (`facebook.com/puregold.shopping`).
- **Price Accuracy vs. In-Store:** Puregold markets no concierge/personal-shopper fees on the Sally app (and P-Wallet free-delivery promos), so app baseline prices closely mirror walk-in retail — **except** for app-only Sally's Deals and Aling Puring wholesale case/pack pricing ("Kitang-Kita Packs"), which require reseller membership.
- **Legal/ToS & Technical Constraints:** No public web surface exists to crawl; reverse-engineering private mobile app APIs violates ToS and breaks silently.
- **$0 Sustainability Verdict:** Puregold is one of the highest-value Philippine value supermarkets for PresyoCheck shoppers, so it **must** be supported through **crowdsourced shelf/receipt reporting + curated promo flyer ingestion**, not web scraping.

#### Robinsons Supermarket (`robinsonssupermarket.com.ph`; former GoRobinsons / GoCart)
- **Platform & Catalog Availability:** **Key finding:** Robinsons Retail's proprietary e-commerce storefronts are **discontinued**. `GoRobinsons.ph` (launched 2020) was rebranded to `GoCart.ph` in 2022 and subsequently sunsetted; live probes confirm `https://gorobinsons.ph` fails with an SSL wrong-principal certificate mismatch (`SEC_E_WRONG_PRINCIPAL`), while `https://www.gorobinsons.ph` and `https://gocart.ph` fail TLS handshakes (`SEC_E_ILLEGAL_MESSAGE`). Robinsons Supermarket's active domain (`https://www.robinsonssupermarket.com.ph/`) has **no e-commerce product catalog**; online grocery fulfillment shifted to third-party aggregators (MetroMart, Pickaroo, GrabMart, Foodpanda).
- **Deals & Promos Visibility:** `robinsonssupermarket.com.ph` hosts marketing sections at `/catalogs` (e.g., `/catalogs/green-tags`, seasonal Christmas catalog campaigns) and `/promos`. Live HTML inspection of `/catalogs/green-tags` confirmed **zero structured HTML price tokens** (`₱`/`PHP` absent) — promos are presented as visual catalog campaigns/assets.
- **Price Accuracy vs. In-Store:** Because Robinsons relies on third-party delivery apps for digital ordering, online prices on aggregator apps carry delivery-platform markup risk (see Aggregators section) and exclude certain in-store Green Tag / Go Rewards loyalty promos.
- **Legal/ToS & Technical Constraints:** `robinsonssupermarket.com.ph/robots.txt` disallows `/api`, `GPTBot`, and `ChatGPT-User`, and pages load Google reCAPTCHA.
- **$0 Sustainability Verdict:** No first-party web catalog exists. Cover Robinsons Supermarket via **crowdsourced receipts/shelf tags + weekly promo flyer parsing** (Green Tags / Go Rewards flyers).

#### WalterMart (`waltermartdelivery.com.ph`)
- **Platform & Catalog Availability:** Powered by **WordPress + Freshop (NCR Voyix)** (`openresty`). Product browsing loads dynamically via client-side Freshop widgets tied to branch selection. Notably, `robots.txt` references `freshop_sitemap.xml.gz`, which currently returns a Google Cloud Storage `NoSuchKey` XML error (`prod-us-freshop-bitpoke-boost/direct/waltermart-freshop-ncrvoy-7oh3/wp-content/uploads/freshop_sitemap.xml.gz`).
- **Deals & Promos Visibility:** Strong public promo presence at `https://www.waltermartdelivery.com.ph/promos` ("Everyday Savings") plus Payday Sale banner memos (e.g., Nov 27–30, 2026 Payday Sale graphic observed on homepage).
- **Price Accuracy vs. In-Store:** **High accuracy.** WalterMart Delivery explicitly advertises a **"same in-store price"** guarantee with no personal-shopper fee (delivery fees/vouchers apply separately, with same-day delivery before 4:00 PM).
- **Legal/ToS & Technical Constraints:** `https://www.waltermartdelivery.com.ph/terms-of-use/` **explicitly prohibits** *"any robot, spider, site search/retrieval application, or other manual or automatic device or process to retrieve, index, 'data mine'..."*.
- **$0 Sustainability Verdict:** Legally off-limits for automated scraping despite strong price parity. If included in v1 (primarily strong in Metro Manila & Luzon), rely on manual seed/flyer ingestion and user receipts.

### 2. Membership Warehouse Clubs

#### Landers Superstore (`landers.ph`)
- **Platform & Catalog Availability:** Express (Node.js) SPA behind **Cloudflare**. `https://www.landers.ph/robots.txt` is permissive (`Allow: /`, disallowing `/customer/*`, `/checkout/*`, `/my-membership`) and points to `https://www.landers.ph/sitemap.txt`, which lists **18,736 URLs** (last-modified Nov 4, 2026), including grocery staples, promo packs, and Landers Marketplace items. However, live HTTP `GET` requests to product URLs return a **40 KB client-side SPA shell containing zero HTML prices and zero JSON-LD**; pricing hydrates dynamically via JS.
- **Deals & Promos Visibility:** High promotional cadence ("Super Crazy Sale," 50% off membership promos, B1T1/promo packs like `2+1` bundles visible across `sitemap.txt`, plus delivery-only deals).
- **Price Accuracy vs. In-Store:** Official metadata confirms **"same in-store prices"** for core superstore inventory, **with two major caveats for PresyoCheck:**
  1. **Hard Membership Gate:** Shoppers must hold an active membership (**₱800/year** Premium/Personal, ₱400 extension; **₱1,000/year** Business) with auto-renewal unless cancelled ≥7 days before expiry — non-members cannot walk in or buy online.
  2. **Online-Only Marketplace Mixing:** `landers.ph` blends physical warehouse grocery SKUs (including private-label **Founders** substitutes like Founders Hazelnut Spread) with third-party **Landers Marketplace** items available *only* online.
- **Legal/ToS & Technical Constraints:** Cloudflare protection, client-side SPA hydration, membership-linked checkout, and standard e-commerce anti-scraping clauses.
- **$0 Sustainability Verdict:** Valuable for PresyoCheck's cross-brand substitute use case (e.g., Landers Founders vs. Nutella), **provided** all Landers prices display an explicit **"Membership Required (₱800/yr)"** badge and exclude online-only Marketplace listings. Source via curated seed + promo flyers + member receipt uploads.

#### S&R Membership Shopping (`snrshopping.com`)
- **Platform & Catalog Availability:** Server-side rendered storefront behind **Cloudflare** with an empty/permissive `robots.txt` (`Disallow:` blank). Crucially, live inspection revealed a **split visibility pattern**:
  - The **homepage** publicly renders featured products with **both `Member Price` badges and `Non-member Price` amounts** (e.g., `&#x20B1; 523.95`, `&#x20B1; 891.45`).
  - **Individual product pages** (`/product/...`) rendered `select-sr-membership-shopping-club` with **zero price tokens** until a warehouse club branch/login session is established.
- **Deals & Promos Visibility:** Clear public promo taxonomy directly linked in site navigation: `/category/on-sale`, `/category/on-sale/buy-x-take-x` (Buy 1 Take 1), `/category/on-sale/price-off`, `/category/on-sale/item-of-the-week`, and `/page/events-and-promotions` (Members' Treat / seasonal sales).
- **Price Accuracy vs. In-Store:** Official S&R FAQs state **in-club and online prices are identical**. However, S&R enforces a **₱700/year** Gold membership (₱900/yr Business; ₱400 supplementary), and online checkout with an expired/non-member status adds a **5% surcharge** (reflected in the displayed `Non-member Price`).
- **Legal/ToS & Technical Constraints:** Despite the empty `robots.txt`, `https://www.snrshopping.com/page/terms-and-conditions` contains **explicit anti-scraping prohibitions**:
  - Clause (6): *"you will not access the Site through automated or non-human means, whether through a bot, script, or otherwise;"*
  - Prohibited Activities (9): *"Engage in any automated use of the system... or using any data mining, robots, or similar data gathering and extraction tools."*
- **$0 Sustainability Verdict:** Do **not** scrape `snrshopping.com` (direct ToS breach + branch-gating + Cloudflare). If included in v1, source via curated flyer parsing (Members' Treat / B1T1 promos) + member receipt uploads, displaying clear Member vs. Non-Member (+5% online) pricing tags.

### 3. Hard Discounters & Convenience Stores

#### Dali Everyday Grocery (`dali.ph` & `dalidash.ph`)
- **Platform & Catalog Availability:** Dali is overwhelmingly **offline-first** for everyday retail shoppers. Its corporate WordPress website (`https://dali.ph/`) contains **only** static informational pages and a store locator (`page-sitemap.xml`) — zero product catalog, zero prices, and zero online promo pages.
- **Key Nuance — DALI D@sh (`https://dalidash.ph/`):** Dali operates a live, publicly crawlable **Shopify** storefront (*"DALI D@sh: Bulk Grocery Orders. Reserve and Collect."*; `robots.txt` explicitly states `Public product, collection... HTML is crawlable`, `Allow: /`). However, live inspection of `sitemap_products_1.xml` revealed major limitations:
  - **Tiny bulk-only catalog:** Only **96 product URLs total** — a small subset of Dali's ~300–500 physical store SKUs (missing many single-piece pantry staples, fresh/frozen meat, and Dali's rotating impulse/private-label finds).
  - **Wholesale carton/case pricing only:** Items are sold by the carton/case (e.g., `Blanca Coffee Twin Pack`, `og:price:amount = 1,080.00` for `53g x 108`, with `Price Per Pc: ₱10` in the product description).
  - **JSON endpoint rate-limited:** Direct requests to `dalidash.ph/products.json?limit=5` immediately returned `local_rate_limited`.
- **Deals & Promos Visibility:** Regular retail markdowns and seasonal promos are distributed **in-store** and on social media posters, not on `dali.ph` or `dalidash.ph`.
- **Price Accuracy vs. In-Store:** D@sh per-piece breakdowns approximate Dali's everyday low prices for bulk-eligible SKUs, but cannot capture single-item shelf changes or store promos.
- **Legal/ToS & Technical Constraints:** Even though `dalidash.ph` allows respectful HTML crawling under Shopify's standard terms, 96 bulk SKUs cannot represent Dali in a shopper comparison app.
- **$0 Sustainability Verdict:** Dali is a **must-have** chain for budget-conscious Philippine shoppers (especially for cross-brand private-label substitutes like Salut, Gonutt, Bakakult), and **cannot be solved by web scraping**. It is the strongest argument for making **receipt OCR + crowdsourced price updates + promo poster parsing** a first-class ingestion path in PresyoCheck.

#### Alfamart Philippines (`alfamart.com.ph`)
- **Platform & Catalog Availability:** **Zero first-party online catalog.** `https://www.alfamart.com.ph/` (Next.js on Google Frontend) exposes only **6 static corporate URLs** in `https://alfamart.com.ph/sitemap.xml` (`/`, `/lets-do-business`, `/work-with-us`, `/franchise`, `/privacy-policy`, `/faq`).
- **Deals & Promos Visibility:** Alfamart's primary promo channels are **bi-weekly/monthly promotional posters** ("Super Sulit" / "Super Savers") published on the official **Alfamart Philippines Facebook page** and branch-level **Viber broadcast groups**.
- **Price Accuracy vs. In-Store:** Partial Alfamart product assortments appear on **Shopee** (mostly multi-pack/bundle items), **GrabMart**, and **Foodpanda Shops**, all subject to platform fees/markups and aggregator WAF/ToS restrictions.
- **$0 Sustainability Verdict:** Completely offline-first. Requires **crowdsourced receipts/shelf reports + Gemini/OCR parsing of bi-weekly Facebook/Viber promo flyers**.

#### 7-Eleven Philippines (`7-eleven.com.ph` & `cliqqshop.com`)
- **Platform & Catalog Availability:** Split across two distinct web properties:
  - `https://www.7-eleven.com.ph/` (WordPress): Lists 391 product marketing posts (`wp-sitemap-posts-products-1.xml` — mostly Chef Creations, City Blends, ready-to-eat meals) and 558 promo posts (`wp-sitemap-posts-promos-1.xml`), **without structured shelf prices**.
  - `https://cliqqshop.com/` (Nuxt SSR, ~935 KB HTML): **Live** e-commerce storefront (*"CLiQQ Shop formerly CLiQQgrocery"*) embedding structured `sku`, `price`, `sale_price`, and `on_sale` fields in SSR payloads (e.g., `sku:"PRDH-4318", price:250.56, sale_price:84.84`).
- **Deals & Promos Visibility:** High digital promo activity across `7-eleven.com.ph/promos/*`, CLiQQ App loyalty deals/points, and CLiQQ Shop discounts (advertising up to 80% off + extra 20% off via CLiQQ Wallet).
- **Price Accuracy vs. In-Store (Critical Warning):** `cliqqshop.com` is a **warehouse-fulfilled, ship-to-store pickup service** limited to Greater Metro Manila and select Luzon branches. Its heavily discounted online prices **do not match walk-in 7-Eleven convenience store shelf prices**, which typically carry a convenience premium over supermarkets and vary by branch/region.

- **$0 Sustainability Verdict:** Do **not** conflate `cliqqshop.com` with walk-in 7-Eleven shelf prices. Either defer 7-Eleven from v1 (since convenience stores rarely win grocery-basket comparisons outside urgent single-item runs) or cover walk-in prices strictly via crowdsourcing and clearly segregate any CLiQQ Shop deals as "Online Store-Pickup Only."

### 3. Crowdsourced Price Reporting & Store Promo Flyer Capture

- **Why It Wins Legally & Strategically:** User-owned receipts, user-observed in-store shelf tags, and publicly distributed print/social promotional flyers avoid every e-commerce anti-scraping ToS clause, capture **true walk-in shelf prices** (eliminating aggregator markups), and provide **equal first-class coverage for offline-first chains** (Dali, Alfamart, Puregold, Robinsons).
- **Promo Ingestion Workflow (Low Effort, High Impact):** Philippine chains publish predictable weekly/payday/monthly promo posters on official websites and social channels (SM Price Drop / Payday Bonus, Puregold Sally's Deals, Robinsons Green Tags, Dali weekly posters, Alfamart bi-weekly Super Sulit posters, Landers Super Crazy Sale, S&R Members' Treat). On a $0 budget, a **curator/community flyer uploader** feeding poster images into `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` with strict JSON Schema output + a 1-minute human review queue can keep active deals fresh across 6–8 chains using <50 API calls/week.
- **Cold-Start & Trust Guardrails (Inputs for Spec/Architecture):**
  - **Day-1 Seed:** Pre-populate 150–300 high-frequency Filipino pantry staples + private-label substitutes (seeded from DTI May 2026 SRP PDF + cleaned Open Food Facts PH dump + manual shelf audit of target chains) so the app delivers immediate value before crowd volume ramps.
  - **Freshness & Decay:** Display explicit provenance & age on every price (e.g., *"Receipt-verified 2d ago · Savemore Quezon Ave"*), auto-expire promo deals at their flyer end date, and visually demote/flag regular prices older than 21–30 days.
  - **Outlier & Anti-Spam Protection:** Auto-flag crowdsourced prices deviating >±40% from the DTI SRP (for BNPC staples) or the chain's rolling median, require a shelf-tag or receipt photo for anomaly overrides, and weight trusted repeat contributors higher.

## Cross-Cutting Price Accuracy Pitfalls to Model in PresyoCheck

Regardless of which chains are selected in ticket [#6](https://github.com/Wynteriall/presyo-check/issues/6), the data model and UI must handle four Philippine market realities surfaced in this audit:

1. **Delivery / Online Markup Distortion:** Aggregator prices (GrabMart, Foodpanda, MetroMart, Pickaroo) frequently diverge by **15%–33%** from walk-in shelves, while `cliqqshop.com` discounts diverge downward by up to 80% from walk-in 7-Eleven stores. **Rule:** Never mix delivery-aggregator or online-warehouse prices into walk-in shelf comparisons without an explicit channel label.
2. **Membership & Loyalty Gating:** Comparing a Landers (**₱800/yr**) or S&R (**₱700/yr**, or +5% non-member online surcharge) price directly against Puregold or Dali without disclosure misleads non-member shoppers. Similarly, SMAC, Puregold Perks / Aling Puring, Go Rewards, and CLiQQ Wallet promos are gated discounts. **Rule:** Model `eligibility` (`public`, `paid_membership`, `free_loyalty`, `wholesale_min_qty`) as a first-class attribute on prices and deals.
3. **Branch, Format, & Regional Variance:** Even within one corporate parent, prices and pack tiers shift between formats (Savemore vs. SM Hypermarket wholesale tiers) and regions (Metro Manila vs. provincial freight; DTI's own May 2026 SRP splits Luzon vs. Visayas/Mindanao). **Rule:** Scope price observations to a `store_branch` (or `city/region` fallback) rather than assuming a single flat national chain price.
4. **Pack-Size & Promo-Mechanic Normalization:** Philippine shelves mix single sachets, twin-packs (`Blanca Coffee Twin Pack`), wholesale cartons (`dalidash.ph` `53g x 108`), and promo packs (`2+1`, `Buy 1 Take 1`, `+20% free`). Combined with noisy external metadata (e.g., OFF `5l` on 55g noodles), PresyoCheck must store normalized `net_content_value` + `net_content_unit` (`g`, `mL`, `pcs`) and compute **effective unit price** (accounting for B1T1 / bundle multipliers) strictly from validated fields.
